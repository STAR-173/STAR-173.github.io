Privacy Policy — gws-personal-cli

Last updated: 2026-10-25

gws-personal-cli is a personal command-line tool used solely by its developer.

What the app accesses

The app accesses the developer's own Google account data (such as Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and other Google Workspace services) through Google's OAuth 2.0 flow, with access scoped to the developer's own account.

What the app does NOT do

  • It does not collect, transmit, store, or share any user data with any third party or external server.
  • It does not use data for advertising, analytics, or profiling.
  • All credentials and tokens are stored locally on the developer's machine and encrypted at rest.
