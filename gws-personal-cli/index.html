gws-personal-cli

A personal command-line tool for accessing Google Workspace services (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, and more) from the terminal.

About

gws-personal-cli is a private, personal-use command-line application. It authenticates with Google via OAuth 2.0 and operates only on the developer's own account data. It runs entirely locally; no data is sent to or stored on any third-party server.
